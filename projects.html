Thermal systems · BUET

Thermo-Drain

Wastewater heat recovery — design, fabrication, and testing

Developed a copper heat exchanger prototype to recover heat from household wastewater and preheat incoming water. In a four-person team, my contributions included SolidWorks design, copper-coil fabrication, and thermal analysis.

  • Achieved 15% heat recovery from domestic wastewater to preheat incoming water; thermal analysis estimated ~3 kW of recoverable heat.
  • Pressure-tested the fabricated copper coil at 150 psi with no observed leakage, demonstrating its integrity under the test conditions.

SolidWorks · Heat transfer · Prototype fabrication · Pressure testing

Robotics & Mechatronics · BUET

Spiderbot

Quadruped robot for automated seed dispensing

Designed and fabricated components for a 12-degree-of-freedom walking robot as part of a team project. Combined SolidWorks modeling, 3D printing, and Arduino control to integrate robotic movement and seed dispensing in a working prototype.

  • Integrated 13 servo motors: 12 for leg movement and one for the seed dispenser.
  • Demonstrated programmed zigzag movement across a 4 × 4 test grid, dispensing one plastic ball per cell to simulate seed placement.

SolidWorks · 3D Printing · Arduino/C++ · Servo Control · Prototype Testing

Energy systems · BUET

Solar PV & Second-Life Batteries

System modeling and battery-storage assessment

Modeled a 2.8 kWp grid-connected solar system in PVsyst to evaluate second-life battery storage for peak shaving. Compared configurations with and without storage using site-specific weather data, annual energy output, and system-loss analysis.

  • Predicted 3,772 kWh/year with battery storage at an 86.1% performance ratio.
  • Quantified a 1.24% reduction in annual energy output from storage integration, identifying the trade-off associated with battery charging and discharging losses.

PVsyst · Solar PV Modeling · Battery Storage · Energy Yield Analysis · System-Loss Assessment
